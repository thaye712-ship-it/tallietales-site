Drive Log Support

Support for Drive Log, an iPhone app published by Tallie Tales LLC.

piledrivingapp@tallietales.com

Email us with questions, bug reports, or feature requests. We reply within two business days.

Before you write

Including these details gets your issue resolved on the first reply:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The Drive Log version number (shown on the app's settings screen)
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see

Common questions

Do I need a signal or Wi-Fi to use Drive Log?

No. Drive Log runs entirely on your iPhone and does not need a network connection. It works in a hole, under a bridge, or anywhere else the bars run out.

Where are my logs stored?

On your iPhone only. There is no account and no cloud server. See the privacy policy for details.

What happens to my logs if I delete the app or lose my phone?

Deleting the app deletes the logs stored inside it. Because the data lives only on your device, we cannot recover it for you. If your iPhone is set to back up to iCloud or a computer, your Drive Log data may be included in that backup and restored with it. Export finished logs off the device if you need to keep them.

Can I get my logs off the phone?

Yes — use the export option on a completed log to send it out through the standard iOS share sheet (email, Files, AirDrop, and so on).

What iPhones does it run on?

Drive Log supports iPhone running a current version of iOS. The minimum supported version is listed on the App Store product page.

I think a count is wrong.

A log can be reviewed and corrected before it is finalized. If you have found a case where the count does not match what happened, please email us with the details above — that is a bug and we want it.

Can you add a feature?

Ask. We build this for people doing the work, and field requests are how the list gets made.

Reporting a privacy concern

Send privacy questions to the same address: piledrivingapp@tallietales.com. Our full policy is on the privacy page.

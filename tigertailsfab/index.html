04 About Us

Tiger Tails Fab is a small family shop that grew out of a love of making things. We design parts that solve real problems around the shop, print gifts and gadgets, and crochet when the printer needs a break.

Most of what you see here comes off our Bambu Lab H3C, so multi-color prints are no problem. Our own designs are free to download on MakerWorld. We post timelapses, tips and the occasional spaghetti monster on YouTube and Instagram, plus an ongoing snow cat build.

Every item here is made when you ask for it. We don't take orders through the site yet: email us, and we'll talk about colors, size, price and timing.

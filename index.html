iPhone app

Drive Log

A field tool for recording pile driving blow counts — built for the person standing at the rig with a clipboard.

Drive Log replaces the paper pile driving log. You tap out blow counts as the hammer runs, the app tracks penetration by the foot, and you leave the site with a clean record instead of a wind-blown, mud-streaked sheet to transcribe later.

What it does

  • Count blows per foot or per set with a large, glove-friendly tap target
  • Track penetration, driving resistance, and refusal against each pile
  • Keep pile, project, and rig details attached to every log
  • Review and correct a log before it leaves the field
  • Export a finished log for the project record

Your data stays on your phone

Drive Log has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. Every log you record is stored on your iPhone and nowhere else. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data — read the full privacy policy.

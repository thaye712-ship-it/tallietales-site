Pile Boss

It counts the blows for you

Pile Boss listens to the hammer through the microphone and counts each blow as it lands — no tapping, no clicker, no losing count when someone asks a question. A live trace on screen shows every hit as it happens, so you can see it keeping pace with the hammer instead of trusting it blind.

Stroke is shown live for every blow and averaged by the foot, straight from the sound of the hammer, so the log carries stroke the way the form asks for it without anyone reading a gauge or guessing.

All you do is press MARK FOOT — one big glove-sized button. The phone buzzes and, if you want, speaks the foot aloud so you never have to look down. Count in feet, tenths, inches, or your own interval, and switch mid-drive when the pile slows.

The record

  • Projects, structures, substructures and piles laid out the way the plans are, with the inspector, hammer, pile type, cutoff, weather and temperature on every pile
  • Driving criteria set once per substructure — blows per foot, minimum stroke, penetration or tip elevation — and a clear meets / does not meet verdict on every pile that updates if the criteria change
  • Initial drive, redrives and restrikes on one log; splices, pile runs, fuel-setting changes, depth corrections and notes stamped where they happened
  • An editable drive log — fix a blow count or an ending depth on the phone, and the record keeps both the original and the correction. Nothing is ever silently overwritten
  • Job info for the whole form: engineers, contractors, cushion and the rest, entered once and printed on every pile. Weather and temperature are chosen per pile, because they change

The forms

Every pile exports as a PDF and as an Excel workbook on the agency's own layout — measured off the blank form, with the header, the foot-by-foot table and the notes where the form puts them. Export a structure or a whole project and the workbook comes out with one tab per pile.

FDOT 700-010-60, GDOT, LADOTD Log of Pile Driving, NCDOT, SCDOT 700.15, TDOT, UDOT Pile Driving Record, VDOT and WSDOT 450-004A, plus a plain record for any other owner. Send files anywhere your phone shares to — email, Drive, Files, AirDrop. More states are coming; ask for yours.

Hammers

A catalog of the most popular diesel hammers — Delmag, APE, Pileco, ICE, Junttan, Bermingham, Vulcan, Conmaco, BSP, Bruce, Liebherr, Kobelco and more — with ram weight, rated energy and stroke ready to check against the data plate. Hydraulic and air/steam hammers are in there too, and you can add your own in thirty seconds.

What it is not

Pile Boss is not a PDA or dynamic testing system. It does not measure driving stress, energy transfer or capacity, and it does not replace the engineer's judgement or the contract documents. The decibel figure it shows is uncalibrated — it exists to help you set the threshold, not to measure sound.

Tallie Tales LLC is not affiliated with or endorsed by any transportation agency. The form layouts are reproduced so an inspector can fill them in; they are not approved submissions.

Your data stays on your device

Pile Boss has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of ours. Every log you record is stored on your own phone or tablet and nowhere else. The microphone is used only to hear the hammer — audio is analysed on the device as it happens and is never recorded or stored. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data. Buying the app, and the optional Pro subscription, are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; they take the payment, we never see a card number. Read the full privacy policy.
